## Dog food dispenser with Arduino Uno.

### Hour 1

Started assembling the cardboard together to construct the dispenser body. Basic structure began coming together, but I knew from the start that some parts would
likely need adjusting once the testing began.

### Hour 2 - 3

Ran into the hardest part of the build; making adjustments to key parts of the design. This was specifically with about the opening at the bottom of the dispenser 
and the lever mechanism. Had to rework more than once to get them functioning properly.

### Hour 4

Hit a big bug during the testing - my dog's food pebbles were too big to fall through the dispenser opening as originally sized. Had to go back and widen the funnel at the bottom to let the kibbles pass through easily.

### Hour 5 - 6

Continued refining the widened funnel and retesting to confirm kibble flowed through consistently without jamming.
