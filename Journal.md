## Dog food dispenser with Arduino Uno.

### Hour 1

Started making up the cardboard together to construct the body of the dispenser. This simple structure slowly began coming together, but I knew from the start that some parts would likely be adjusted once the testing began later on.

### Hour 2 - 3

Ran into the hardest part of constructing the entire prodect; making adjustments to key parts of the design. I had to adjust the dimensions in the opening at the bottom of the dispenser and the lever mechanism. Had to rework more than once to get them functioning properly.

### Hour 4

Hit a bug when I was testing out the dispenser - my dog's food pebbles were too big to fall through the dispenser opening as originally sized. Had to go back and widen the funnel at the bottom to let the kibbles pass through a lot more easily.

### Hour 5 - 6

Continued refining the funnel and retesting to confirm kibble flowed through easily without jamming all the time!
